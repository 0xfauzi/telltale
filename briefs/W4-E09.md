Pre-registered decision rule, written here before the run:
- A probe is "answered" when the median precision and the median recall over its 3
  repetitions are both 1.0; "partially answered" when the median recall is 1.0 and the
  median precision is below 1.0; "not answered" otherwise; "not scored" when the runner
  reports unknown on 2 or more repetitions (the result text could not be read).
- A measure (the 22 of spec 13.7 plus the two scores) is "resolvable at n = 3" for a
  probe when MDD <= 0.25 median (6.12's demotion rule); otherwise the report prints
  N_needed and says "not resolved at n = 3", never "no difference".
- Nothing in E09 compares probes to each other as better or worse repositories; the
  probes are one repository under six questions, and the comparison E10 needs is
  between commits, not between questions.
