Two lessons from E09 that this brief turns into mechanism:
- E09's STOP rule could not act: the pilot ran one probe three times and the suite ran
  every repetition in one invocation, so four sessions crossed the token bound and
  nobody saw it until the end. Here the runner runs REPETITION-MAJOR: repetition 1 of
  every probe in every arm (four sessions), then repetition 2, and so on, and after
  every session compares its wall and its token total (every kind, as `telltale show`
  sums them) with the spec's `stop` block; the first session over a bound ends the run,
  the report says which session and which bound, and nothing else is started. The
  first round is the pilot and needs no separate spec.
- Precision is an upper bound and a key can be narrow. E09's P3 and P5 keys were; the
  E10 keys carry the files the code confirms and the provenance note says so.
