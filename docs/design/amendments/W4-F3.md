# W4-F3 design amendment

Lines for the wave 4 amendment block of `docs/design/01-design.md`, folded by the
orchestrator at the wave 4 gate.

- 6.11 (W4-F3): a verification number whose inputs include a run with no observed
  outcome is NULL, not a count over the runs that had one. Four numbers depend on an
  outcome and all four take the rule: `failed_test_runs`, `fail_to_pass_cycles`,
  `edits_after_last_successful_test` and `post_failure_revisits`. `agent_test_runs` and
  the two epoch counts do not, because "the agent ran the tests" is not a statement
  about how they ended. The scope is the metric's own: `failed_test_runs` asks it of the
  test runs and the other three of every verification run.
  `measures_intervals.outcomes_known(runs)` is the one place the question is asked, and
  `failed(run)` is what it asks, so the rule cannot drift between the four.

  W3-T3 got half of this. It stopped a masked run's status being read as the check's,
  and then printed the count over what was left: `failed_test_runs: 0` beside a warning
  saying all eleven runs were masked. 0 over nothing is not a smaller measurement of the
  same thing, it is a different statement, and it is the one a reader acts on.

  What forced it, measured on the E12 material store (`experiments/E12/out/store`, six
  build sessions) and on the E12 pilot (`docs/experiments/E12.md`): the arm S reviewer,
  reading only `telltale show`, `timeline` and `vector` for W4-T1/1, answered "0 failed
  pytest runs" where the raw stream holds 6, "yes, an edit after the last pytest run"
  where the raw stream says no, and "0 tool errors" where the raw stream has 1. Every
  one of the six captures has every one of its test runs masked:

      capture     runs  masked  tests  tests_masked  stated  is_error results
      W4-T2/1       38      35     10            10       3                 3
      W4-T1/1       21      20     11            11       1                 1
      W3-E08b/1     10       7      6             6       3                 1
      W3-V/1        12      12     11            11       0                 6
      W3-E08/1      27      20      2             2       7                 4
      W3-T4/1       16      15     11            11       1                 1

  After the rule, all four numbers are null on all six, at coverage `partial`.
  `experiments/E12/make_key.py --check --material` goes from 31 of 36 agreements to 30:
  the one that leaves is W3-E08/1's Q2, where the key's 0 met a 0 computed over two
  masked runs. That agreement was a coincidence of the value and not evidence of the
  reducer being right.

- 6.11 (W4-F3): coverage stays `partial` and never becomes `unavailable`. The runs WERE
  observed; their outcomes were not, and `unavailable` is the word for a capability no
  surface could show. `measures_spec13._stated` decides the word, and it now asks
  `outcomes_known` rather than looking for a `success` or an `exit_code` field on the
  row: since the 6.10 amendment below a masked row carries `success`, and a `_stated`
  that read the field would have printed `observed` over the captures whose value had
  just become null. `_stated` takes the extra capability names of the metric asking it,
  so `edits_after_last_successful_test` and `post_failure_revisits` still weaken on
  paths and commands as they did, and the outcome rule and the capability rule stay one
  answer rather than two that could disagree.

  The source list of a null is every run that was read and never the empty set of
  failures: `telltale explain <cap> failed_test_runs` on W4-T1/1 walks to all 11 runs
  and their observations. An Evidence with no source is refused by the model.

- 6.11 (W4-F3): the masked warning gains one sentence, `of the K runs whose outcome a
  surface stated, J failed`, with K and J counted over the metric's own scope. A reader
  told only `null` cannot tell a session whose 11 runs were all masked from one where a
  single masked run hid an otherwise complete picture, and both are in the six captures
  above: W3-V/1 states 0 of 12 and W3-T4/1 states 1 of 16.

- 6.10 (W4-F3): a masked chain is MARKED and then read like any other call. The row
  still says `exit_masked`, and it now also says what the call itself did, from the same
  three routes an unmasked call uses (OTel `tool_result.success`, a PostToolUseFailure
  hook event, the stream's `is_error`). Before this the reducer returned before reading
  any of them, so a tool that errored on a masked chain recorded nothing at all.

  The exit code stays OFF the row, which W4-T3 decided and this does not touch: the
  observation holds it and `explain` reaches it. What changed is one field, and the
  reason the two are not the same decision is that the exit code of a chain is another
  program's status while `is_error` is the call's own.

  Measured on the six captures: 4 of their 16 `is_error` tool_result rows sit on a
  masked chain, and all four were dropped. W4-T1/1's is `set -e ; git checkout _ _
  src/telltale/experiments_env.py ; python3 _ << _ _ ; uv run ruff check . && uv run
  ruff format --check . && uv run mypy . 2>&1 | tail -2`, whose tool_result is an error;
  the reviewer answered "0 tool errors" for a session whose timeline had no failed row
  in 202.

- 6.10 (W4-F3): the timeline word for a masked row is the call's word followed by
  `, check masked`, and `check masked` alone when no surface stated the call's outcome.
  Unmasked rows do not change. W4-T1/1's outcome column goes from 74 `ok`, 128 `-` and 0
  `failed` to 74 `ok`, 108 `-`, 19 `ok, check masked` and 1 `failed, check masked`. The
  two facts a person needs are the two halves of the word: the call ran and exited 0 (or
  did not), and nobody saw how the check inside it ended.

- 6.12 (W4-F3, a consequence handled where it landed): `series._State.consume` reads
  `success` off a verification_run to keep `last_verification_failed`, and the 6.10
  amendment above put that field on masked rows. It now passes None for a masked row, so
  the column is exactly what it was: a run whose result nobody stated leaves the last
  stated answer standing. Measured on the same six captures with and without the guard:
  the column is identical on all six with it, and without it W3-E08/1's failure is
  cleared five rows early, 16 rows of `last_verification_failed` 1 becoming 11, because
  the chain that followed ended in `tail` and `tail` exited 0.
