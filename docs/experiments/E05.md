# E05: how much does a measure move when five real sessions run the same task?

Why this matters (30 seconds): every comparison this system will ever make between two
repositories, two models or two efforts is a difference between two numbers that also
move on their own. H2 asks how far they move when nothing varies except the run. Until
that floor is measured, a difference of 12 per cent between two repositories could be
the repositories or it could be Tuesday, and design 6.12's demotion rule has nothing to
compare against.

**The answer is in "The measured condition (E05r2)" below.** Five sessions, one task,
one environment: of the 28 measures reported, 22 did not move at all, five moved and
every one of them resolves at n = 5, one could not be judged for want of a value, and
NOTHING is demoted. The re-run passed acceptance five times out of five.

It took two conditions to get there. The first five sessions, on 2026-09-02 under task id
`E05`, ran with a permission mode that stopped the agent from running any command, so
they measured the spread of a permission failure rather than of the task. That condition
is kept below, under "The pilot condition (E05)", because it is the record of what was
spent and because the defect it found is now a refusal in the runner. **The two
conditions are never in one table.** They differ in one launch flag and in their base
commit, and each has its own task id in the store.

## Decision rule, written before the run

Copied verbatim from the brief for W2-E05, which was written before any session started,
and stored in `experiments/E05/out/decision.json` under `rule` so the two cannot drift.

> Per measure over the five captures: n, median, s (scaled MAD), MDD = 2.8 s sqrt(2 / n),
> demoted from repository comparison while MDD > 0.25 median,
> N_needed = ceil(2 (2.8 s / (0.25 median))^2). s = 0 means "no within-condition
> variation observed at n = 5", neither demoted nor endorsed. median = 0 with s > 0 is
> demoted with N_needed "not computable at median 0". A measure with fewer than 5 known
> values reports its n and is not judged. Scaling to N = min(20, N_needed) is NOT this
> task: the report lists N_needed per measure and the orchestrator takes the list to the
> owner.

The pre-registered constants of design 6.12 travel with every table:
`2.8 = z_{0.975} + z_{0.80}`, relative resolution `0.25`, scaled MAD `1.4826 MAD`, n = 5.

### Why every session failed

From the store alone, identically in all five captures:

- 3 `claude.stream.assistant` messages carrying a `tool_use`, all of them `Bash`. Zero
  `Read`, zero `Edit`, zero of anything else.
- 3 `claude.stream.system.permission_denied` observations.
- In the four sessions that produced a result message: `is_error` false, `stop_reason`
  `end_turn`, `num_turns` 4, and `permission_denials` listing all three `tool_use` ids
  with `tool_name` `Bash`.
- `telltale show` on any of them prints `"permission_mode": "acceptEdits"` in the
  session block.
- The three `verification_run` activities carry `command_norm` `uv run pytest _ >& _ |
  tail -50` once and `uv run pytest` twice, `category` `test`, `success` false, and NO
  exit code.

`--permission-mode acceptEdits` auto-accepts file edits and nothing else. A `Bash` call
under it raises a permission request, and in a headless `-p` run there is nobody to
answer one, so the call is refused and the model is handed an error instead of output.
Three attempts, then the session stopped. E01's S1 ran this same prompt with
`--permission-mode bypassPermissions --max-turns 40` and fixed the test in 7 turns
(`experiments/E01/run.py`, `build_argv`), so the flag is the whole difference.

**`agent_test_runs` 3 and `failed_test_runs` 3 are three refused tool calls, not three
failing test runs.** `activities._tool_outcome` takes `success` from OTel
`tool_result.success`, from a `PostToolUseFailure` hook, or from the stream's
`is_error`; a permission denial sets `is_error`, so `success` is false by the same route
a failing test sets it. The two are distinguishable in the capture, through
`claude.stream.system.permission_denied` and through the absent exit code, but not from
the `success` field alone. Nothing here is a defect in the reducer: it recorded what the
surfaces said. It is a caveat on any reading of `failed_test_runs` on its own.

## What it means

**The pilot is invalid as a measurement of H2, and the reason is one flag.** H2 asks how
much a measure moves when the same task is done five times. No session did the task.
Five sessions asked to run a command, were refused three times each, and stopped. The 18
measures with no variation have none because every session did the identical nothing:
zero files read, zero files changed, zero searches, zero compactions, three refused Bash
calls. A floor computed from that is the floor of a permission failure. Using it to
demote or endorse a measure for repository comparison reads the harness's configuration
as a property of the work.

The one demotion the table does produce, `stable_state_work.stable_state_total_ms` with
N_needed 132, is real about these captures and useless as guidance: the numbers behind it
(3125, 4092, 7983, 8188, 33274 ms) are how long five refused sessions sat between the
launcher's snapshots, and attempt 3 sat 33 s for a reason nothing here measured.

**The harness itself is not what failed.** Every part of the recorder did its job on
five real sessions: five captures, 4/4 surfaces each, one environment fingerprint across
all five, attempts 1..5 correlated to the task, five acceptance results the harness ran
itself, and a session summary that prints the offending `permission_mode` in plain
sight. The condition was one condition; it was the wrong condition.

## What we rejected

**"The agent failed the task."** It was never allowed to attempt it. The store shows
three `tool_use` blocks, all `Bash`, and three `claude.stream.system.permission_denied`
observations against them; no `Read` and no `Edit` was ever issued, and the result
message names all three refused `tool_use` ids under `permission_denials`. A model that
tried and failed leaves reads, edits, or a test run with an exit code. This one leaves a
request for permission and nothing after it.

**"The model was slow or unstable, because attempt 3 took 38 s against attempt 1's 8.6
s."** All four completed sessions did the same four turns and the same three refusals,
and their token totals agree to within 0.3 per cent (135316, 135347, 135449, 135677).
The spread is in `duration_ms`, not in the work, and what it measures is API latency on
an otherwise identical exchange.

**"Attempt 5 is a sixth kind of failure."** Attempt 5 is the same failure with fewer
turns in it: 3 `claude.stream.system.permission_denied` observations, 3 Bash `tool_use`
blocks, `exit_code` 143 from the harness's own teardown. It is reported separately
because it is truncated, not because it is different.

**"Rerun it and the numbers will be right."** Five new sessions is a new spend and an
owner decision. Nothing here was rerun.

## What downstream may now say

Nothing about H2. No within-condition floor was measured, no measure is endorsed or
demoted for repository comparison on this evidence, and the numbers in the tables above
may not be quoted as run-to-run variation of the fix-the-test task. They may be quoted,
as derived per-capture numbers and comparative-within-condition statistics, about
exactly one cohort: five `claude -p --model sonnet --permission-mode acceptEdits`
sessions of E01's fix-the-test prompt on 2026-09-02, base commit
`cb4bfa27e68c2adc8aa3a4cf35fed5b590bf9a29`, environment
`env_0752834a9fffca335a83c115d5c4f0598288637c0b00d0e6262d9c62fd7b6054`, content level 1,
in which no session was permitted to run a command. Coverage caveats: `pre_compaction_tokens`
and `read_to_edit_ratio` have no value in any of the five, `duration_ms` and `num_turns`
have four of five, and attempt 5 is a truncated capture.

What E05 does support, and it is not nothing: the launcher, the receiver, the four
surfaces, the fingerprint assertion, the correlation and outcome records, the reducers
and the evidence vector all ran correctly against five real Claude Code sessions, and
the capture is complete enough that the defect in the experiment is diagnosable from the
store alone without opening a transcript.

## What it means

The floor is low. Repeating one small fix-the-test task five times under one environment
leaves 22 of the 28 reported numbers exactly where they were, and moves the other five by
amounts far below design 6.12's quarter-of-the-median resolution. On this task, a
between-condition difference of more than a quarter of the median in any of these
measures is not run-to-run noise.

The measures that moved are the ones that measure the model rather than the work:
`output_tokens` (595 to 656), `cache_read_tokens` (155,995 to 156,087) and the agent's own
`duration_ms` (11,460 to 12,982 ms), plus the two `stable_state_work` numbers that are
functions of them. Everything counted from what the agent DID (files read, files changed, edits,
searches, test runs, turns, tool calls) was identical in all five.

That is a stronger result than the protocol needed, and it comes with a caveat the next
section states: it is one small task, and a task with more choices in it will not
necessarily behave this way.

## What we rejected

**"22 measures with zero spread means those measures do not vary."** It means five
samples cannot see their spread. This task admits essentially one solution shape: read
the file, guard the zero, run the tests. A task with more branches in it leaves the agent
room to read three files in one session and one in another, and nothing here measures
that. The verdict string says "no within-condition variation observed at n = 5"
for that reason, and it is not an endorsement.

**"The agent solved it the same way five times."** Four of five produced a byte-identical
patch; attempt 4 produced a different one (+3/-3 against +3/-6, a different `diff_hash`).
The measures did not separate them, and the repository snapshot did.

**"`failed_test_runs` 0 means the first test run passed."** It did not. The fixture
starts with one failing test, and every session ran the tests before editing. The agent
piped pytest into `tail`, so the shell's exit status is `tail`'s 0, and both
`verification_run` activities record `success` true. `fail_to_pass_cycles` is 0 for the
same reason. Nothing in the reducer is wrong: it recorded the exit status of the command
that was run. The caveat is on the measure, and it is the mirror image of the pilot's
(where three refused calls were recorded as three failed test runs). Reading
`failed_test_runs` as "tests that failed" is unsafe in both directions whenever the agent
wraps its test command in a pipeline.

**"The re-run and the pilot can be compared, since only the flag changed."** They differ
in the flag AND in the base commit sha, and the pilot's fifth session was truncated by a
harness teardown. Two differences and a truncated capture are not one factor. They are
two conditions with two task ids, and design 6.12's between-arm statistics were not run
on them.

## What downstream may now say

Within this condition, comparative: the run-to-run spread of E01's fix-the-test task
under `claude -p --model sonnet --permission-mode bypassPermissions --max-turns 40`,
content level 1, base commit `d8cca120d0d938ef798e6795b35af4676d6be06a`, environment
`env_7fff8b24f0a5356d8bbed1bc2333afe1f0a9fabf170e487968ca5396144f8602`, five captures on
2026-09-02. No measure of the 22 in spec 13.7's vector is withheld from repository
comparison on this evidence, and the five measures that moved are resolvable at n = 5
with `N_needed` 1.

The coverage caveat is one metric: `pre_compaction_tokens` has no value in any of the
five, because no session compacted, and a sum over an empty set is null rather than 0.

Three limits on the claim, each of which needs its own measurement before anyone leans on
it. It is ONE task, and a small one: the floor of a task with more choices in it has not
been measured. It is one repository and one model on one day; H3 varies the model and the
effort and has not been run. And "no variation at n = 5" is a statement about five
samples, not about the measure.

## NEXT

`N = min(20, N_needed)` scaling is not indicated for any measure here: every measure that
moved has `N_needed` 1. What the H2 protocol needs next is a SECOND task, larger than
this one, to find out whether the floor stays this low when the work has choices in it.
That is an owner decision and a new spend.
